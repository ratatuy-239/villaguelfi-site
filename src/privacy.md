## What this website does

The website presents Villa Guelfi and its apartments. It has no booking form, no user accounts and no online payments.

Bookings are made on Airbnb or Booking.com. When you follow a “Book” button you leave this website, and your booking data is handled by Airbnb or Booking.com under their own privacy policies. We receive from them the information needed to host you.

## Personal data we process

### When you contact us

If you write to us by email, WhatsApp, Telegram or Instagram, we process your name, your contact details and the content of your message, in order to reply and to arrange your stay. Please do not send sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for your request.

Messages sent through WhatsApp, Telegram and Instagram are also processed by those services under their own privacy policies.

### When you visit the website

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. (USA). To deliver the pages and keep the service secure, GitHub automatically processes technical data such as your IP address, browser and device type, the pages requested and the date and time of access. We do not have access to these logs and cannot identify you from them.

### Statistics (only with your consent)

If you accept cookies, we use Google Analytics (Google Ireland Limited) to see, in aggregated form, how many people visit the website and which pages they view. Google Analytics uses cookies and processes online identifiers, including a shortened IP address, and information about your device and your visit. If you decline, Google Analytics is not loaded at all.

### Map

The map of the area is provided by Google Maps. It is loaded only after you accept cookies or press “Show the map”. Once loaded, Google receives your IP address and may set its own cookies.

## Why we process data and on what legal basis

- **Replying to your messages and arranging your stay** — steps taken at your request before entering into a contract, and performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
- **Delivering the website and keeping it secure** — our legitimate interest in running a working, safe website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
- **Visitor statistics and the embedded map** — your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time.
- **Legal obligations** — where the law requires us to keep or disclose information, for example guest registration and tax rules (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use it for automated decision-making or profiling.

## Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small files that a website stores on your device.

- **Necessary.** The website itself sets no cookies. It only remembers your cookie choice in your browser's local storage (entry `vg-cookies`), so that the banner is not shown on every visit.
- **Statistics.** Google Analytics cookies (`_ga`, `_ga_*`, kept for up to 2 years) — set only if you press “Accept”.
- **Third parties.** Google Maps may set cookies once the map is loaded.

The fonts used on this website are stored on the website itself and are not loaded from third parties.

You can change your choice at any time:

You can also delete or block cookies in your browser settings.

## Who receives the data

Personal data is shared only with service providers that are needed to run the website and to communicate with you: GitHub (hosting), Google (statistics and map, only with your consent) and the email and messaging services you choose to contact us through. Data may also be disclosed to public authorities where the law requires it.

## Transfers outside the European Economic Area

GitHub and Google may process data in the United States. Such transfers are based on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or on the Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission.

## How long we keep data

- Messages and contact details — for as long as needed to handle your request and your stay, and afterwards only as long as the law requires or as needed to deal with possible claims.
- Statistics — according to the retention period configured in Google Analytics, up to 14 months.
- Your cookie choice — in your browser until you change it or clear your browser data.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your personal data, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing carried out before the withdrawal.

To exercise your rights, write to [{{ site.email }}](mailto:{{ site.email }}).

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Italy this is the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali ([www.garanteprivacy.it](https://www.garanteprivacy.it)).

We may update this policy if the website or the law changes. The current version is always available on this page.
